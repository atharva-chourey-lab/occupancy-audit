# The Brain Does Not Fill the Skull: A 42-Species Occupancy Audit Finds Ruler Chaos, Not a Constant

Neuroanatomy · 2026

Author: Atharva Chourey
Affiliation: Atharva Chourey Lab (independent research lab)

*Manuscript — not yet peer-reviewed.*

## Abstract

Fossil encephalisation quotients rest on an identity: that endocranial volume (ECV) — the volume inside the skull — equals brain volume, an occupancy of 100%. This paper joins the two literatures that measure the two sides of that identity and computes occupancy directly. Under a pre-registered protocol, we joined endocast volumes (Dembitzer et al., 2022; Waugh & Thewissen, 2021; Sakai et al., 2016) to brain volumes (the Patzke et al., 2015 compilation; the same sources' paired values; Brain Catalogue MRI) for 42 mammal species — a far smaller overlap than either literature suggests. Median occupancy is 0.919 (IQR 0.824–0.973), but only 40.5% of species land inside the 90–100% band the identity implies, and 19.0% exceed 100% — physically impossible values that diagnose ruler mismatch rather than biology. The cleanest arm, 26 species with brain and ECV from the same source, gives a median of 0.920 with none above 100%; a bridge arm pairing modern endocasts with the histology-lineage brain compilation gives a median of 1.010, with 60% of species above 100%. Whole orders sit outside the band (Artiodactyla 0.79, Cetacea 0.79, Perissodactyla 0.84), and the largest modern ECV dataset silently back-calculates endocast volume from brain volume for 149 of its species — an assumed ~90% occupancy wearing a lab coat. The honest headline is double: the brain fills roughly nine-tenths of the skull in the median mammal, and the field's rulers disagree by more than the shortfall, so no single occupancy constant survives contact with the joined data.

*Keywords:* endocranial volume; encephalisation quotient; occupancy; comparative neuroanatomy; measurement; pre-registration

## Key results

- **Median occupancy 0.919 — and only 40.5% of species inside the assumed band.** Across the 42-species join, 59.5% of species fall outside 90–100% occupancy; 19.0% exceed 100%, which is physically impossible and diagnoses ruler mismatch, not biology. Nothing was trimmed.
- **The cleanest arm:** 26 species with brain and ECV from the same source (Waugh & Thewissen, 2021) give median 0.920 (IQR 0.845–0.949) with none above 100% — the folk "endocasts run 8–12% large" correction is about right for the median.
- **The bridge arm breaks:** pairing modern endocasts with the histology-lineage brain compilation gives median 1.010, with 60% of species showing brains bigger than their skulls — the Two Rulers offset swallowing the occupancy signal. The lion's occupancy is 1.02 on the histology ruler and 0.77 on the MRI ruler.
- **Whole orders sit outside the band:** Artiodactyla 0.79, Cetacea 0.79, Perissodactyla 0.84 (medians). The bowhead whale sits at 0.323 — an outlier within its own order, not a clade condition (beluga 0.792, bottlenose dolphin 0.937).
- **The largest modern ECV dataset assumes the answer:** for its 149 brain-mass species, published "Endocast Volume" is back-calculated as brain volume × 1.108 — an assumed ~90.2% occupancy, silently encoded.
- **Scoped claim:** fossil EQs carry an unacknowledged ruler-dependent error of at least ±10–25% before any biology enters. No per-fossil correction factor and no replacement constant is licensed — that refusal is part of the result.
